Making a plan: three ways in, one sheet

Replaces today’s New plan → Pick a date → Pick a time steps. The screens below are each way in.
1 · WAYS IN
Planner
  • Tap an empty time → sheet opens with that time
  • Or + button → next half hour, 1 hr
  • No one invited yet
Discover / activity
  • “Make it a plan” on an activity
  • Activity title, photo and usual length filled in
  • You pick friends
Group chat
  • “Plan something” in a chat
  • Everyone in the chat is invited
  • Plan lands in that chat
2 · ONE SHEET
Create sheet
  • Same screen for every way in, just pre-filled differently
  • Time is set on a mini “your day” right in the sheet: drag the block (T2)
  • Friends’ busy shows as gray stripes with their initials
  • All day → day range instead of times
  • “Counts as busy” switch
  • No friends → Save · friends → Send
3 · WHAT HAPPENS
Just me → personal event
  • Shows on your planner in its color
  • Drag / resize it on the planner any time
  • Tap it → same sheet to edit or delete
With friends → proposal
  • Card in the chat + a ghost on everyone’s planner
  • Friends answer Going / Can’t make it within 24 hrs
  • First “Going” confirms it · nobody → expires
4 · CHANGING A GROUP PLAN
Suggest a new time → vote
  • Group plans can’t be dragged (long-press says why)
  • “Suggest a new time” opens the sheet in suggest mode
  • Vote in the chat: Keep current time vs new time
  • Works on proposed and confirmed plans
  • No votes or a tie → keeps the current time
