## If `*1.3` is deferred

It is the only part of Project 1 that needs a paid Apple account, so deferring it is reasonable. What that costs:

- **Nothing else in the roadmap breaks.** Projects 2–21, including the core loop of 15, 16 and 17, never touch it.
- **No App Review problem while deferred.** Guideline 4.8 only applies once a third-party login is offered at all. **Google and Apple ship together or neither ships.**
- **1.4 is the only ticket affected**, and the workaround is cheap: build its sequence to *accept* a pre-filled identity even while email/password is the only thing supplying one.
- **Do not defer 0.2 with it.** 0.2 needs no paid account — a free Apple ID covers the simulator and a personal device — and it is what stops iOS breakage accumulating silently. Already visible: `'Anonymous Pro'` and `'Inter'` are referenced across the screens with **no font files in the repo and no asset-link config**, so both platforms are quietly falling back to system fonts.
